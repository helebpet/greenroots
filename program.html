Program

We're hosting three specialists from the Bay Area: Yolanda Burell, an Oakland-based farmer and owner of Pollinate Farm; Bonnie Rose Weaver, a medicinal herbalist and owner of 1849 Medicine Garden; and Priya Anand, founder of Fog Hollow Ferments.

Schedule:

  1. 10:30 AM

    Welcome speech

  2. 11:00 AM

    Workshop,

    sustainable gardening and soil health with Yolanda Burell

  3. 12:30 PM

    Break

  4. 1:30 PM

    Workshop,

    hands-on herbal medicine lecture with Bonnie Rose Weaver

  5. 2:30 PM

    Medicinal tea party with Bonnie Rose Weaver

  6. 3:00 PM

    Fermentation tasting with Priya Anand

  7. 3:45 PM

    A walk around the SF Botanical Garden

  8. 4:30 PM

    Q&A with the guest speakers

  9. 5:30 PM

    Final goodbyes and wrap up

Yolanda Burell

Pollinate Farm

Works with California universities and students, including interns from Stanford and UC San Diego, and collaborates with local farmers' markets and restaurants.

Bonnie Rose Weaver

1849 Medicine Garden

An herbalist, farmer, and educator specializing in herbal medicine, especially urban medicinal gardening.

Priya Anand

Fog Hollow Ferments

Founder of a small-batch fermentation kitchen, leading a hands-on tasting built around what ferments well straight from the garden.

Sunday, April 18, 2027 · Botanical Garden, Golden Gate Park

All proceeds from tickets and merchandise support Pollinate Farm & Garden and 1849 Medicine Garden.
