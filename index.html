Our mission

Rooted in soil, grown in community.

Green Roots is a benefit event promoting sustainable gardening and the everyday value of herbal medicine. Every workshop, lecture, and conversation is designed to help you help the planet, and your health, at the same time. We're partnering with Pollinate Farm & Garden and 1849 Medicine Garden to bring hands-on permaculture practice to the San Francisco Bay Area.

Transparency

Where your ticket goes.

Every dollar raised funds hands-on garden work in the Bay Area. Here's exactly how this year's ticket and merchandise sales are put to use.

  1. 40% Community garden grants for growers like Pollinate Farm
  2. 25% Herbal medicine education with 1849 Medicine Garden
  3. 20% Venue, program, and event production
  4. 15% Seed library and next year's benefit

Why Green Roots

Three voices, one movement.

We're hosting three specialists from the Bay Area: Yolanda Burell, an Oakland-based grower and owner of Pollinate Farm; Bonnie Rose Weaver, a medicinal herbalist and owner of 1849 Medicine Garden; and Priya Anand, founder of Fog Hollow Ferments.
