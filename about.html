About Green Roots

Est. San FranciscoNon-profit

Green Roots teaches sustainable gardening around San Francisco and shows you how to use herbs as medicine.

We started as a few neighbours swapping seedlings over a fence. Now we run one benefit event a year, and every dollar it raises goes to two small Bay Area farms.

Food can be a poison, but it can also be medicine; it depends on the quantity and the quality.
Green Roots, founding principle

The story

Most people never meet the person who grows their food. What you eat shapes your health more than almost any other choice you make in a day. Farms that skip pesticides, chemicals and GMOs give you food worth eating.

Paying growers near you keeps that food within reach. Herbs work the same way. Grown well and used with care, they handle small complaints and cost the land nothing.

That is what the event is for: growers, herbalists and neighbours in the same garden for one day.

The people behind it

Yolanda Burell

Farmer · Owner, Pollinate Farm & Garden

Yolanda farms in Oakland and owns Pollinate Farm & Garden. She grows food, feed and flowers on an urban homestead, and she will show you that a backyard, a balcony or a strip of sidewalk is enough to start with.

At the event she leads the small-plot growing lecture and the afternoon soil clinic.

Bonnie Rose Weaver

Herbalist · Owner, 1849 Medicine Garden

Bonnie is a herbalist and owns 1849 Medicine Garden. She grows and wildcrafts the plants she makes medicine from, all within a few miles of the city. She also teaches people to name what is already growing around them.

At the event she runs herb identification and the tincture-making workshop.
